Staying private

XMPP

Only room moderators can see your real address. Everyone else only sees your nick.

IRC

Your IP is never shown, everyone gets a cloaked host. If you log in with an alo.ne account your account name is visible, so for these rooms connect without logging in and use a nick that isn't your username.

Technically, we can see: everything said in these rooms, like anyone in them can. It's kept for 30 days. We don't log IP addresses. Full details at legal.alo.ne.
