The alo.ne website and API go through Cloudflare (a US company) for protection against attacks. The chat server, the mail servers, this page and the code check don't: you connect to them directly.

02

What we never keep

  • IP addresses. Not in logs, not in the database. Mail and chat servers run with logging off.
  • Access logs or tracking. No analytics, no ad trackers, no fingerprinting.
  • Your passwords in readable form. Dashboard passwords are stored as a one-way argon2id hash.
03

Dashboard and account

Account

Username, a hash of your password, your 2FA secret if 2FA is on, membership and storage limits, email address and aliases if you use email, biolink profile and badges, referral and reseller records, invite codes, credits and items, and your Discord ID and username if you link Discord.

Biolink views

Your IP is used briefly, in memory only, so one visit counts once. It's never saved.

Technically, we can see: everything listed above. Cloudflare sits in front of the website and API, so it sees your IP and the requests you make there. Your passwords (dashboard, email, webmail and chat) are encrypted in your browser with a key only our server holds before they're sent, so Cloudflare only sees scrambled data. That code is itself delivered through Cloudflare, so you can check it hasn't been changed at check.alo.ne, which doesn't go through Cloudflare. Cloudflare does see the short-lived (5-minute) login tokens your browser sends with each request.
04

File host

Files sent through chat are deleted after 30 days, and you can delete any file whenever you like. Upload dates are hidden if you choose "remove timestamps".

Files

Your files, their names, sizes and types, and the folders and shares you create.

Encrypted uploads

Encrypted in your browser before upload. We only ever have scrambled data, and the key stays in your link.

Reports

If someone reports an encrypted file and includes its key, our moderators can open that one file, and we keep the key for moderation.

Technically, we can see: normal uploads, and the names, sizes and types of all files. We can't open encrypted uploads without the key. The key stays in the part of the link after #, which browsers never send to us or to Cloudflare.
05

Email

Mail servers keep no logs by default. If we need to debug a specific problem, we turn logging on for a set time (usually 2 hours), and it's wiped when it switches off. Outgoing mail has your app name and your IP removed from its headers.

Storage

All mail sits on encrypted disks. With encrypted storage turned on, new mail is also encrypted with your own key, which is locked with your mail password.

Backups

Taken every 6 hours, encrypted, and kept for up to 12 months. Mail you delete can stay in older backups until they expire. With encrypted storage on, your mail stays encrypted inside the backups too.

Technically, we can see: mail stored without encrypted storage, while the servers are running. Senders, recipients and subjects of mail passing through. Your mail password when you log in, because the mail server has to check it. With encrypted storage on, we can't read stored mail without your password, but a server that was changed to capture passwords could. Webmail encrypts your mail password before it's sent, so Cloudflare can't read it, but our server decrypts it to log in to the mail server for you.

Encrypted DMs (OMEMO)

Turn on OMEMO and your DMs and files are encrypted on your device. The server only ever sees scrambled data.

Chat password

Stored only in a form that can check it, never readable. Apps prove they know it without sending it.

Bridged channels

#general and #suggestions are copied to Discord, Telegram and IRC. #premium is copied only to premium-only channels on Discord, Telegram and our IRC. Those copies follow those platforms' rules, not ours. DMs are never copied.

Other servers

You can talk to people and join rooms on other XMPP servers. Anything you send there is also handled by that server and follows its rules, not ours. With OMEMO it only gets scrambled data, but it still sees your address and when you message. Your IP address is never passed on. Our member channels are only open to alo.ne accounts.

Public rooms

#lobby and #pl (privacy & legal) are open to anyone, on XMPP and on irc.alo.ne, and are copied between the two only. Anything said there is public and kept for 30 days like our other channels. On XMPP only moderators can see your real address. On IRC, if you log in, your account is visible to others, so connect without logging in and use a different nick for more privacy. #pl is people sharing experience, not legal advice.

Checking our code

We publish signed hashes of the webchat and dashboard code on GitHub, Discord and Telegram. Check them yourself at check.alo.ne or with the commands in each signed message. For the strongest protection, use an app such as Conversations, Monal or Gajim.

Technically, we can see: who messages whom, as it happens, because the server has to know where to deliver each message. We don't log it. We can also see the content of channel messages, and of DMs and files sent without OMEMO, for as long as they're kept above. That includes messages to and from people on other servers, which pass through ours on the way.
07

Payments

Crypto

We keep the order, the amount and the receiving address we generated for it.

Cards

Handled by Mollie. We never see your card number. Mollie keeps what card processors are required to keep.

Payouts

For referrals and sales, we keep the amount, the currency and the transaction ID.

Technically, we can see: your orders and payment history with us, and anything public on the blockchain about the addresses involved.
08

Legal requests

We only respond to valid legal process from a court or authority with jurisdiction over the server involved, and we only hand over what this page says we have. Where the law allows, we tell the affected user first. We can't hand over what we don't keep: no IP addresses, no access logs, no OMEMO-encrypted content, and no DMs or channel messages past the times above.

Like any provider, we can be legally ordered to change what we collect from then on. If that ever happens, this page and our announcements are where you'd see it, as far as the law lets us say.
